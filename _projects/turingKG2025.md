\### 4th Annual Symposium of the Turing Interest Group on Knowledge Graphs  

\*\*Date:\*\* 12 September 2025  

\*\*Location:\*\* City St George’s, University of London

\*\*Time:\*\* 10:00 – 18:00 BST 



The \*\*4th Annual Symposium\*\* of \[The Turing Interest Group on Knowledge Graphs](https://www.turing.ac.uk/research/interest-groups/knowledge-graphs) focused on \*The Turing Grand Challenges\*, with a particular emphasis on \*\*Health and Medical Transformation\*\*. The symposium featured keynotes, short talks, poster sessions, and networking activities designed to promote interdisciplinary exchange across academia, industry and government. A special session was devoted to \*\*“Neurosymbolic AI for Medicine” (supported by the \*\*Network of the Academy of Medical Sciences (AMS)\*\*). This session showcased the Network activities and aimed at promoting synergies with the Turing Interest Group on Knowledged Graphs. 





\*\*Sponsorship\*\* played an important role in enabling the event. The symposium was supported by \*\*Oxford Semantic Technologies\*\*, \*\*Mind-Alliance Systems\*\*, and the \*\*AMS Network on Neurosymbolic AI for Medicine\*\*. Their contributions ensured a technically rich, inclusive event and helped highlight the importance of integrating knowledge-graph and neurosymbolic methods into medical research agendas under the Turing Interest Group umbrella.
